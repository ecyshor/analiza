## What is Analiza?

{{ .Site.Params.hero.subtitle }}

Analiza is open source on GitHub: https://github.com/ecyshor/analiza
App (sign in): https://app.analiza.dev
Contact: support@analiza.dev

All data is stored and processed in the EU (Hetzner, Germany). The tracker sets no
cookies, uses no localStorage and no cross-site/cross-device tracking, so the
analytics itself needs no cookie banner, and there is no fingerprinting code.

Your data is yours: from the Export page in the dashboard (https://app.analiza.dev)
you can request a full export of all your analytics data at any time, on every plan
including Free, and we email it to you once ready. The full stack is also open source
(https://github.com/ecyshor/analiza) and can be self-hosted with Docker Compose
(`make start` per the repo README); the tracker's `hostname` attribute sends events
to your own API endpoint instead of api.analiza.dev.
